# Future features (V2 / V3 ideas — NOT implemented in V1)

These are ideas for later versions. None of them exist in the V1 codebase.

1. **Automatic transaction detection from SMS** – read bank/UPI SMS messages and turn them into draft transactions.
2. **PhonePe / Google Pay transaction recognition** – recognise payments made through UPI apps.
3. **Payment notifications** – e.g. "We detected a ₹450 UPI payment. Add it to SmartSpend?"
4. **AI spending analysis** – explain unusual spending and highlight patterns.
5. **AI-generated monthly financial summary** – a short written recap each month.
6. **AI category prediction** – suggest a category from the note or merchant.
7. **Receipt OCR** – scan a receipt photo to fill in amount, date and merchant.
8. **Budgets** – monthly limits per category with progress tracking.
9. **Recurring transactions** – rent, subscriptions and salary created automatically.
10. **Savings goals** – target amounts with progress.
11. **Bank integrations** – import transactions through a bank/aggregator API.
